# 2026-09-15 — classify at the boundary that still has the evidence

A status after a wrapper may have lost its cause: pnpm turns shell 127 into exit 1 while its
structured lifecycle record retains ENOENT. An existence predicate can similarly erase EACCES.
Use the underlying structured error or direct read before interpreting absence/failure.

The real controls and mutation output are in mutations.txt; initial typecheck and build failures
are retained rather than rewritten as successful runs. Adding a third status is only useful when
controls prove the production entrypoint reaches it and real failures remain failures.
