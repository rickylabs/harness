## Contract

Orchid native-run read protocol version 2 adds two optional record fields, `launch` and `taskStart`.
Each is `{ at, generation }`: a canonical millisecond UTC instant, and the source generation whose
process clock sampled it. That is the generation at capture, not at the read.

- `launch` is the job's original launch instant. It is recorded after the seat registers and the
  vendor process starts, and persisted before goal delivery. Reads, restart and resume never advance
  it. It is omitted for adopted and legacy runs, and frozen into the completion fence.
- `taskStart` is persisted once, when Remote Control Codex native acceptance certifies the first
  accepted turn from typed app-server evidence. Every other vendor omits it; a typed vendor
  task-start signal is tracked upstream in Herdr #5725.

Cockpit's strict client reads only version 2 (Cockpit doctrine rule 11), so the two must be
deployed together. Orchid is optional enrichment: until both are live, the observer runs
Herdr-only. Cockpit records `orchid_launch` and `native_start` through the exact native assignment
alias join, on clock domain `orchid-native:<generation>`. A latency is measured only between
instants of one generation. Duplicates are idempotent, older frames change nothing, a changed value
becomes a conflict and is never renewed, and an Orchid restart re-serves the same points unchanged.
The two gaps are removed from the capability map.

## Evidence and acceptance boundary

Both repositories ran their full configured local gates and compiled red/green controls: 12 Orchid
mutants and 9 Cockpit controls, each red on an assertion and green when restored. Root-only Orchid
kernel lanes are unproven locally and unchanged. One different-family evaluator session (matrix
sol, `gpt-6.1-sol`, high) was re-steered over the joint trees. Round 1 was FAIL_FIX, because the
generation-change test did not change the generation; it was fixed. Rounds 2 and 3 were PASS on the
exact heads listed in each PR. Full results are in those PRs.

No deployment, service restart, dispatcher-setting change or live launch/Stop was made. No deployed
stage percentile or live endpoint is claimed.
